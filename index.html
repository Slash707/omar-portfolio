Electronic Control System for Multi-Source Renewable Energy

Wind speed changes constantly, and so does the electrical power it generates. A control system has to absorb those variations and coordinate energy storage so the output stays stable. My project examines how multiple renewable sources, particularly wind, can work with battery storage and intelligent control to supply reliable power. One subject I have studied is fuzzy logic control for a wind and battery energy production system.

  • Renewable energy system architecture
  • Wind energy generation and battery energy storage
  • Fuzzy logic control and energy management
  • Power distribution and voltage stability
  • Control strategies for variable energy generation
Scope of my work: technical research and analysis. I have studied engineering papers, system architecture, and control strategies. I have not built, implemented, programmed, or tested the complete control system.
